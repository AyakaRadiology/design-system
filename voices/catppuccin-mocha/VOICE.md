# Voice: catppuccin-mocha

The [Catppuccin](https://catppuccin.com/palette) Mocha palette on the house
schema, dark only. `../catppuccin-mocha.css` carries the gate-verified values
(the same floors as every voice).

## When to reach for it

As for `../catppuccin-macchiato/VOICE.md`: a tool someone sits in for hours.
Usually one of the choices an app offers through `themes/catppuccin.css`.

## The rules that make it read as itself

The rules of `../catppuccin-macchiato/VOICE.md` hold unchanged. Mocha is
the darkest flavour: the Macchiato recipe clears every floor on it unchanged, so its only steps off the palette are Macchiato's own (the series); every such value says so in the CSS.

## What this voice is not

Not a variant to edit: a flavour of its own, valued from its own palette.
